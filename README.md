# ☕ Coffee Companion

A simple web app that helps you make the best possible cup of coffee at home on a semi-manual espresso machine, with either a built-in or separate grinder.

Pick a drink and get the beans, grind, dose, shot size, milk, cup size and method, scaled for 1–4 cups.

## Features

- **Nine drinks:** short black, long black, americano, piccolo latte, macchiato, flat white, cappuccino, latte and mocha
- **Per-drink specs:** bean dose (g), shot yield (ml), extraction time, milk and hot water amounts, cup size, foam style and step-by-step method
- **Grinder toggle:** guidance for built-in or separate grinders
- **Cup scaler:** quantities for 1–4 cups
- **Beans guide:** how to buy, which roast suits which drink, and how to store beans
- **Dial-in & fixes:** a grind-adjustment table for sour, bitter, fast and slow shots
- **Care guide:** cups, milk jug and a machine cleaning schedule
- Light and dark mode, mobile friendly
